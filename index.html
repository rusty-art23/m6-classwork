Work with your partner. Do the three missions, then answer the exit ticket on your own and tap Send to teacher.

Mission 1 · about 8 minutes

Build the life cycle

Choose an animal. Tap the four pictures in order, from the start of life to the adult. Then choose the type of life cycle. Do all four animals.

Mission 2 · about 6 minutes

Growth, development and the egg factory

A · Growth or development?

Growth = getting bigger. Development = changing form and function. Choose one for each sentence.

B · How long is the wait?

Match each animal with the time from egg or mating to the baby.

C · The hen’s egg factory

An egg travels through four parts of the oviduct in about 25 hours. Match each part with its job.
